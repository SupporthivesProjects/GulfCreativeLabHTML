Example copy

Why Gulf warehouses are moving inland

Ten years ago, the best warehouse in the region sat as close to the port as the rent allowed. That logic is changing. Land near the coast is expensive, the roads in and out are crowded, and the new freight corridors run the other way.

Operators who moved thirty kilometres inland report lower rents and quicker turnarounds for the trucks that matter most: the ones heading to customers, not the ones arriving from ships.

The trade off is staff. Inland sites need transport for their teams and a plan for the summer heat. The firms doing this well treat both as part of the move, not an afterthought.
